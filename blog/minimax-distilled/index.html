MiniMax-H3 distilled comparison

Comparing merged MiniMax-H3 checkpoints and the base model

Nov 5, 20269 min read

On this page

  • Floating city: text-to-video
  • Rally car: fast motion
  • Ice ring chase: image-to-video

Recently we’ve released a lot of merged checkpoints for MiniMax-H3, linked in the table below. Below we compare some example renders between all of them with a full 20 timestep render. The results are not straightforward and we didn’t find any numerical measures that convey anything meaningful.

I2V times use the rally car shot and Ref2V times use the elf archer shot. We timed warm requests on an RTX 4080.

Settings are the same for every render:

  • 1344×768, 124 frames at 24 fps, audio on
  • CFG 1
  • INT8 ConvRot checkpoints, with the LoRAs merged in
  • SageAttention with 15% Nox sparse attention, first step dense (see the sparse attention post)

All six checkpoints share one architecture, so a denoising step costs the same for each, any difference in timings above is mainly noise.

Floating city: text-to-video

The first shot is a slow dolly into a floating city above the clouds. It has little motion and a lot of fine architecture.

The lightx2v LoRAs are close to the base render in terms of size and architecture. DMAD and PDMD noticeably differ in aesthetics compared to the rest.

Rally car: fast motion

The second shot is designed to have fast motion, where a 4-step sample has the least room to resolve detail.

Similar to the previous shot, the lightx2v variants are closer to the base in terms of looks. The DMAD versions lose much of the detail.

We also rendered the rally car with a shared first frame from Krea 2.

Compared to the T2V renders, this fixes the starting point and all of them now follow the intended environment. The 4 step lightx2v has a strange artifact making the car drive backwards, likely resolved by picking another seed but strange nonetheless.

Ice ring chase: image-to-video

Another different I2V case with the following starting image:

DMAD and PDMD seem to follow the prompt more closely. The base and lightx2v renders do not consistently reduce the number of drones after the explosion.

Elf archer: reference-to-video

The last shot tests the Ref2VA checkpoints with three reference images, all generated with Krea 2: two character sheets and the forest location. The prompt describes three different shots and is quite complex, to test adherence using references.

The renders generally preserve the characters and location from the references, though the scene still feels lackluster for a cinematic short.
